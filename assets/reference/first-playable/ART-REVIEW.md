# Art review — 30 September 2026

18 final references: one unchanged user anchor plus 17 generated boards. Reviewed visually as each built-in generation returned. Two assets required one correction each; 19 generated outputs total. Reference acceptance means useful for the documented purpose with these notes, not perfect multiview correspondence or completed 3D assets.

## Review criteria

Family fit, silhouette at gameplay distance, understandable major shapes, articulation, equipment/side consistency, and manageable detail. Enemy palettes are manufactured graphite/brick-red/amber; hero/outpost are ivory/navy/amber. VFX boards use simplified proxies. Industrial construction boards outrank scenic character insets for environment design.
